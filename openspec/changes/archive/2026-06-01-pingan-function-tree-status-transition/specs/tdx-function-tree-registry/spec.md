## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: FUNCTION_TREE SHALL record the executed PingAn implemented-status transition

`FUNCTION_TREE.md` SHALL mark D-07 and D-08 as `[已实现]` only after an explicit transition record generated by `task pingan-implemented-status-transition`.

#### Scenario: D-07 and D-08 are transitioned with auditable evidence

- **GIVEN** D-07 and D-08 previously registered `pingan-implemented-status-transition-writer`
- **AND** an eligible transition gate exists for target nodes `D-07` and `D-08`
- **WHEN** the guarded transition is applied with `confirm_transition=true`
- **THEN** D-07 and D-08 SHALL have status `[已实现]`
- **AND** evidence SHALL mention `runtime/pingan/d07-d08-implemented-status-transition-record.json`
- **AND** evidence SHALL mention `function_tree_status_transition_executed=true`
- **AND** evidence SHALL mention `pingan-function-tree-status-transition`
- **AND** the boundary SHALL state that the transition does not execute PingAn workflow, submit orders, or create new production trading behavior.
