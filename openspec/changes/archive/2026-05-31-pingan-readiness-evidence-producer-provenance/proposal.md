# PingAn Readiness Evidence Producer Provenance

## Why

The previous promotion gate requires each source evidence file to carry `artifact_provenance`, but the real producer outputs are not yet guaranteed to emit that metadata. That makes the gate useful for blocking hand-written JSON, but incomplete for real evidence generated by `trade preflight`, `trade dialog-readiness`, and the trade audit acceptance coverage reports.

## What Changes

- Add `artifact_provenance` to `TdxTradeManager.pingan.preflight(...)` result data.
- Add `artifact_provenance` to `TdxTradeManager.pingan.dialog_readiness(...)` result data.
- Add `artifact_provenance` to `TdxTaskManager.trade_audit_daily_report(...)` and `trade_audit_period_report(...)` acceptance coverage outputs.
- Update tests, OpenSpec specs, and `FUNCTION_TREE.md` D-07/D-08 evidence and boundary.

## Non-Goals

- Do not execute PingAn buy/sell/confirm workflows.
- Do not submit, retry, or cancel orders.
- Do not mark D-07/D-08 as implemented.
- Do not claim broker production readiness or live/manual acceptance completion.
- Do not relax the artifact provenance gate added by `pingan-artifact-provenance-promotion-gate`.
